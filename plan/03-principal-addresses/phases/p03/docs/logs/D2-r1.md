# 03PA-P03 D2/r1 — Direct-thread company ownership and atomic teardown

> No backward compatibility. Change the init schema; use fresh owned disposable databases.

Original requirements: plan03 phase03/04 and TASK AC5–AC6, principal target invariants in accepted
p02 D1/r3+D2/r2. D1/r3 remains accepted for agent-owned publication receipt cleanup; this extension
addresses a separate newly demonstrated direct-thread teardown gap. No proposed change here has
been implemented. Source identity is the frozen partial B1 context, not accepted implementation.

## Contradictory evidence and responsible ownership

B1-notice-runtime-tests2.txt shows the actual private principal-notice company teardown failing
external_threads_target_principal_fk. Native incoming FK inventory is B1-delete-principal-fks.txt.
Rollback-only B1-teardown-probe1..4.sql/.txt establish two distinct facts on author runtime4:
1. external_threads and threads NO ACTION target-principal pins prevent the tenant's normal cascade
   ordering; deferral of those pins alone merely postpones the failure.
2. threads has a cascading (company,channel) owner FK but no company owner FK. A direct thread's
   nullable channel therefore leaves it alive after company deletion. At SET CONSTRAINTS ALL
   IMMEDIATE, the deferred principal pin correctly refuses the resulting orphan.
3. Adding explicit company ownership with cascade, while deferring the two exact principal pins,
   makes the same delete and all immediate constraint checks succeed before ROLLBACK.
All exploratory DDL/data effects were rolled back; no source or lasting fixture schema change.

## Decision

Give threads an explicit company owner FK (company_id)→companies(id) ON DELETE CASCADE.
This owns both desk and direct thread lifetime; retain both exact same-tenant desk/principal target
FKs and their exact-one target CHECK. Do not cascade thread deletion from principal deletion: a
live company still retains a principal's direct threads and refuses physical principal deletion.
Agent deletion continues to demote a principal, and its application direct-thread/run refusal stays.

Make only the p02-added direct target-principal NO ACTION pins DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED.
Scope is the explicit *_target_principal_fk constraints on threads, thread_messages,
thread_principals (target_principal_id), workflow_message_intents, workflow_message_destinations,
workflow_history_members, workflow_runs, workflow_admission_refusals, agent_history_sessions,
agent_history_conversations, background_tasks, channel_reply_contexts, external_threads,
internal_notes and message_deliveries. Leave existing CASCADE target-principal FKs on
workflow_bindings, channel_reply_settings and notifications unchanged. Preserve every composite
thread/membership/binding owner FK, unique key, policy predicate, immutable origin/receipt guard,
and every unrelated incoming history/publication reference. No blanket SET CONSTRAINTS bypass,
manual company child-table purge, extra principal cascade or data compatibility path.

The target pins keep exactly the same tenant/principal ownership invariant at commit, while actual
row-owner cascades can finish atomically during company teardown. Invalid/foreign/retired targets
still cannot commit. A transaction with direct history cannot commit a physical principal deletion.
Application locking/authorization and before-durable-effects revalidation do not change. Any
concurrent ingress either holds company KEY SHARE first and finishes before company teardown,
or observes the deleted company and refuses; no target/binding/identity lock inversion is added.

## Failure behavior and proof

Keep immediate application invalid-target errors and existing native composite guards. For a test
specifically exercising one of the newly deferred target pins, assert rollback/commit refusal or
explicitly SET CONSTRAINTS ... IMMEDIATE in its transaction; do not weaken the tenant invariant.
Retain partial input and effects on any failure through transaction rollback. A successful company
teardown leaves no direct threads, canonical notice/message/intent/receipt/target/root rows or
principal endpoints belonging to that company, while another tenant's rows remain unchanged.

Required focused checks: real private-notice company teardown (existing worker→admission→root),
company teardown with direct transport/conversation/history/notes/outbox as affected, native foreign
principal insert/update cannot commit, live-company principal deletion with retained direct thread
refuses atomically, and competing company teardown versus direct ingress with the normal lock prefix.
Run fresh migrations and native FK inspection, focused principal history/retention/notification tests;
final p04 full repository/CI gate remains mandatory. AC1–AC6 and all D1/r3 obligations remain.
Architecture PASS accepts this decision only, never runtime or whole-B1 implementation.
