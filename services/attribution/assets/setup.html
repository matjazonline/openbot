    Approved domains

    Add an exact host, then publish its DNS TXT record. Approval lasts 30 days; reverify before expiry. Revocation is permanent for that domain record and its installations.

      Public installations

      Installations allow image, landing and viewport observations after consent. They cannot record sales, refunds or read reports.

        Server API keys

        Secrets are shown once and cannot be recovered. Keep them out of browser scripts. If a response is lost, retry it, then revoke the returned key and issue another if the secret is unavailable.

          Allowed operations
          The old key works for at most this period, capped by its existing expiry. Revoking the replacement does not extend the old key.

          Campaigns and tracking links

          Each tracking link belongs to an ad and placement. Disabling it stops new clicks and preserves its history.
