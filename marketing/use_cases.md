### 1. Inbound Invoices & Vendor Bill Auditing ("Forward & Done")

*Stop manually comparing line items between PDFs, purchase orders, and accounting software.*

* **The Trigger:** A vendor emails a monthly service invoice with an attached PDF (`Invoice_9841.pdf`). An operations manager forwards it to `ap@yourcompany.com`:  
  > *"@ap-agent verify against Q3 PO #1128, validate tax identification, and prepare draft approval."*
* **The Shared Discussion:**
  - The **AP Agent** parses the PDF table, cross-references line items against your **shared company knowledge** (approved vendor master catalog and contract discount terms), and verifies payment IBAN/SWIFT details.
  - The agent runs the **SOP Checklist**: flags that sales tax was calculated at 21% instead of the 19% negotiated rate ($420 discrepancy).
* **People Lead:**
  - The agent drafts an email to the vendor explaining the calculation and requests a revised invoice.
  - The finance manager reviews the draft in one click: **[Approve & Dispatch]**.
* **The Result:** 20 minutes of tedious manual spreadsheet matching reduced to a 10-second review. Zero accounting mistakes slip through.

### 2. Multi-Specialist Customer Resolution (`support+billing+legal@`)

*Turn multi-department customer questions into a single, elegant customer reply.*

* **The Trigger:** An enterprise customer emails asking: *"We need to expand our tier to 200 seats, apply our volume discount, and confirm if data processing terms comply with upcoming EU regulations."*
* **The Collaborative Pipeline:**
  - The email is routed to `support+billing+legal@yourcompany.com`.
  - **Step 1 (Support Agent):** Confirms active plan tier and drafts step-by-step seat provisioning instructions.
  - **Step 2 (Billing Agent):** Retrieves contracted volume pricing from shared company knowledge, calculates the prorated upgrade cost, and notes it in the thread.
  - **Step 3 (Legal Agent):** Ingests the GDPR/DPA clause library and appends our standard compliance certification.
* **The Shared Review:**
  - An Account Executive sees the combined draft, makes one minor personalized greeting adjustment, and approves.
* **The Result:** Instead of three confusing emails from three different departments over three days, the client receives one unified, flawless response within minutes.

### 3. Inbound Sales RFP & Custom Quote Preparation

*Accelerate sales cycles without sacrificing pricing discipline or deal desk rules.*

* **The Trigger:** A prospective client emails a 15-page RFP questionnaire. The sales rep forwards it to `dealdesk@yourcompany.com`:  
  > *"@dealdesk analyze security & functional questions from attached RFP, prepare answers from our knowledge base, and draft pricing for 500 users."*
* **The Discussion & SOP:**
  - The agent processes the attachment, matches 85% of security and architecture questions directly against verified **Shared Company Knowledge**, and marks the remaining 15% as *"Needs Rep Clarification"*.
  - Pricing is drafted strictly according to your sales SOP margin rules.
* **People Lead:**
  - The sales rep reviews the flagged questions, answers them via quick internal notes, and approves the proposal draft.
* **The Result:** RFPs that used to sit on desks for a week are turned around in an hour, giving your sales team an unbeatable first-mover advantage.

### 4. Logistics & Operations Exception Handoff

*Resolve shipping delays and vendor bottlenecks before customers complain.*

* **The Trigger:** A carrier sends a shipping exception notice: *"Shipment #88192 delayed due to customs hold."* The logistics desk receives the notice automatically.
* **The Automated SOP:**
  - The agent identifies affected customer orders, checks inventory at backup fulfillment centers, and prepares two options:
    - *Option A:* Re-route replacement from Dallas warehouse.
    - *Option B:* Send immediate delay apology with expedited delivery credit.
* **Internal Team Discussion:**
  - The operations lead tags the account manager: *"Dallas has 4 units left. Should we re-ship?"*
  - Account manager replies: *"Yes, VIP client. Re-ship."*
* **Deterministic Execution:**
  - The agent's draft is approved. A re-ship request is fired to the warehouse API deterministically, and an empathetic update is emailed to the client.
* **The Result:** Proactive customer care with zero dropped balls.

### 5. Proactive Morning Briefings & Scheduled Audits

*Start every day knowing exactly what needs human leadership.*

* **The Schedule:** Every morning at 7:30 AM, scheduled cron agents execute under authorized supervisor oversight.
* **The Agent's Work:**
  - Audits pending high-priority threads across all company channels.
  - Identifies threads where SLA response deadlines are approaching.
  - Verifies database health, unresolved handoffs, and stalled delivery queues.
* **The Discussion:**
  - The agent opens a fresh morning discussion thread in `/ui` or sends an internal email digest to the leadership team:
    > *"Good morning team! 3 items require attention today: 1 enterprise contract awaiting VP signature, 2 pending vendor approvals, and 0 delivery errors. Have a great day!"*
* **The Result:** Complete situational awareness without digging through six dashboards.
