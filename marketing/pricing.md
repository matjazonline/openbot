## ❓ Common Pricing Questions

#### Do my external customers or vendors count as seats?
**No.** Your clients, vendors, and external partners interact via standard email and are 100% free. You only pay for internal team members who participate in shared discussions and approve drafts.

#### Can we use our own LLM enterprise discounts?
**Yes.** All tiers support BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) with AES-256-GCM encryption. You pay your AI provider directly at your negotiated volume rates.

#### What happens during the Early Access period?
During our early access rollout, we are working closely with selected cohorts. We provide complimentary onboarding and hands-on workflow configuration to ensure your team's success.
