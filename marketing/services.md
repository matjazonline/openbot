# Business Process Services: We Learn How You Work, Then Build Agents for Each Step

> **"Leave busy work to BusyBots and focus on what matters most."**

---

## 🧭 The Problem: Nobody Has Time to Write the Playbook

Most teams know their recurring work could be delegated to agents. What stops them is the work before the work:
1. The procedure exists, but only in the heads of the people who run it every day.
2. Manuals and policies describe the ideal case, not the exceptions that eat the week.
3. Configuring an agent means deciding exactly what it should do, what it may touch, and when a person has to sign off.

**So the busywork stays.** The team that most needs the time back is the team with the least time to design the process.

---

## 🛠️ The Service: We Explore Your Business and Build the Procedure With You

Our team works alongside yours to map a recurring process, write it down as a business operating procedure, and set up custom agents for the tasks inside it. You keep the decisions; we do the groundwork.

### 1. Explore the Business
We sit with the people who do the work, review real requests from your inbox or helpdesk, and read your manuals and policies. The goal is to find where the time actually goes, including the exceptions the manual never mentions.

### 2. Write the Procedure
We turn what we learn into a business operating procedure: each step, the checks that keep it correct, who decides at each point, and where work changes hands between people and agents.

### 3. Build Custom Agents
We create a specialist agent for each task in the procedure. Each one gets its own instructions, reusable skills, the tools it needs and no more, and access to the company knowledge that applies to its step.

### 4. Pilot & Hand Over
We run the procedure on real requests with review before sending enabled, so a teammate approves every draft. We refine what needs it, then hand your team a process it owns and can change.

## 📦 What You Leave With

- **A written operating procedure.** Steps, quality checks, approval points, and handoffs documented in plain language your team can train on and audit.
- **Agents built for your tasks.** Specialists configured around your policies, systems, and tone, instead of general-purpose assistants adapted request by request.
- **Human decisions kept human.** We mark where people approve, decide, or take over, and configure review before sending where a teammate must sign off.
